# Community Code of Conduct

VCRS exists to make software development more approachable and more trustworthy. The community should reflect the same values.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with respect and patience;
- welcome questions from people with different levels of technical experience;
- give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and contributions;
- distinguish evidence from opinion;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them;
- credit sources and prior work;
- protect private, personal, client, and employer information;
- respect stated boundaries and requests to stop an interaction;
- focus disagreements on the work rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained unwanted attention;
- discriminatory, demeaning, or sexualized language or imagery;
- personal attacks, insults, or deliberate humiliation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation or misrepresentation intended to deceive;
- knowingly submitting secrets, private code, client data, or identifying records;
- plagiarism or intentional removal of attribution;
- spam, manipulation, or undisclosed promotional activity;
- repeatedly disrupting a discussion after a moderator asks for a change in behavior.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository Issues, Discussions, pull requests, reviews, release spaces, and other channels operated for the project. It also applies when someone is officially representing VCRS.

## Reporting a concern

For urgent platform abuse or threats, use GitHub's reporting tools.

For a project conduct concern, do not publish sensitive incident details. Open an issue titled **“Private conduct contact requested”** with no additional incident information, or contact an active maintainer through a private method shown on their GitHub profile. A maintainer who is involved in the concern should not handle the report.

## Response

Maintainers may request clarification, remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or ban an account from project spaces. Actions should be proportionate to impact, documented privately where appropriate, and focused on restoring a safe environment.

Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.
